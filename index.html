Our story

A café built for long plans and last-minute ones.

Arabista sits beside the Pal–Umra bridge — an open-air courtyard of green chairs, warm string lights and one of the best sunset views in Surat. Founders sketch pitch decks here. Creators shoot reels here. Friends come for a quick coffee and stay till close.

Behind the bar, a precision grinder doses every shot to the gram, cold brew steeps slowly, and matcha gets whisked to order. In the kitchen: honest Italian comfort, crispy bites and desserts worth the detour.

Come as you are. Your table's waiting.
